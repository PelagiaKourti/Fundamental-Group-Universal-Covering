# Fundamental Group and Universal Covering Spaces

Course project for **Differentiable Manifolds** (MSc in Theoretical Mathematics),
Department of Mathematics, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, January 2024.
Written in Greek.

**Author:** Pelagia Kourti
- 📄 [PDF](./Fundamental_Group_Universal_Covering.pdf)

## Summary

The project develops the theory of the fundamental group and of covering spaces:

- **Homotopy:** homotopic maps, homotopy equivalence, contractible spaces.
- **Fundamental group:** loops and their homotopy classes, the group structure, induced homomorphisms,
  change of base point, and the fundamental group as a functor from pointed topological spaces to groups.
  Includes a proof that the complex projective space ℂPⁿ is simply connected.
- **Covering spaces:** covering maps, morphisms of coverings and deck transformations.
- **Lifting:** uniqueness of lifts, the homotopy and path lifting properties, the monodromy theorem,
  and the lifting criterion f\*(π₁(Z)) ⊂ π\*(π₁(X)).
- **Universal covering:** existence and uniqueness of the universal covering space.
- **Universal covering and the fundamental group:** the isomorphism π₁(Y) ≅ Deck(X → Y),
  and finiteness of π₁ for spaces with compact universal cover.
